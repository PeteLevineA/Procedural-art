Petrova Fractal

A 100% procedural OLED fractal dive — pure GLSL escape-time math rendered as a single fullscreen shader. The camera falls logarithmically into a Julia-set boundary while the parameter c slowly morphs, the plane gently rotates, and a domain-warp term injects organic curl so the viewer feels they are travelling deeper into recursive detail rather than merely zooming in. Inky black voids, neon blue, neon purple and hot magenta filaments, with white-hot peaks where structures pile up. No images, sprites or pre-rendered assets.
