Petrova Wormhole

A fully 3D procedural OLED wormhole ride. The camera flies a parametric spline through a tube of neon-blue, magenta and purple particles arranged in rings, ribbons and bottom-up streamers. Streaks whip past as the track banks like a roller-coaster — then the ride slams to a sudden landing and the last few sparks dissipate back into pure black.
